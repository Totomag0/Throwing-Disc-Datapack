**Throw it, watch it bounce, catch it back.**
A vanilla-style throwing weapon for Minecraft Java **1.21.5 → 26.3**, inspired by the upcoming Throwing Disc, with 8 tiers, ricochets, sword enchantments and 3 brand-new enchantments.

---

## How it works

- **Right-click** to throw the disc in the direction you are looking.
- It flies straight, **ricochets** off walls (up to 8 times, getting faster with each bounce), then **comes back to you** like a Loyalty trident, passing through blocks on its way home.
- It **pierces** through every mob in its path and returns to the hand that threw it.
- **Decorated pots** shatter on impact and drop their contents.
- Uses durability like any tool (no wear in Creative).

## Enchantments

Discs can be enchanted at the **Enchanting Table** and the **Anvil**.

**Sword enchantments:** Sharpness, Smite, Bane of Arthropods, Fire Aspect, Looting (works on thrown kills!), Unbreaking, Mending, Curse of Vanishing.
*(Knockback and Sweeping Edge are not available on discs.)*

**New enchantments:**
- **Grapple (I)** – Grabs one item stack (1 to 99) **or** experience orbs, never both, and brings it back to you, carried on the disc.
- **Colossus (I–III)** – The disc grows during its flight, reaching ×2 / ×3 / ×4 its size when it comes back. Its hitbox grows with it.
- **Tornado (I)** – Pulls nearby mobs toward the disc while it flies.

New enchantments can be found in the Enchanting Table, loot chests and from Librarian villagers.

## Languages

English by default. Translations available in the companion resource pack: **English, French, German, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Russian**.

## Installation

1. Put the datapack in your world's `datapacks` folder.
2. Enable the **Throwing Disc Classic** resource pack for the disc models and translations.
3. Run `/reload`.

**Useful commands**
- `/function throwing_disc:give_all` – get one disc of each tier
- `/function throwing_disc:uninstall` – cleanly remove the datapack before deleting it

## Known limitations

- Fire Aspect and Tornado only affect mobs (players cannot be moved or set on fire by commands).
- Discs can't be thrown while riding a pig or strider.
- Discs are based on Carrot / Warped Fungus on a Stick, so pigs and striders follow players holding one.
